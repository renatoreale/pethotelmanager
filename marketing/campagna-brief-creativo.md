# Brief creativo — nuovi asset visivi campagna cross-canale

**Nota tecnica:** in questa sessione il generatore Higgsfield risultava senza crediti disponibili, quindi le immagini qui sotto non sono state generate direttamente. Il progetto usa già un metodo gratuito e già collaudato per gli asset AI (Pollinations.ai, vedi nota in `piano-60-post.md`) — questi prompt sono pronti per essere usati con quello stesso strumento o con qualunque generatore immagini disponibile.

**Palette brand (da `marketing/social/generate-cards.mjs`):** crema `#fdf7f0` (sfondo) · arancio bruciato `#c45a12` (accento) · marrone scuro `#2b1d12` (testo). Font headline: Georgia/serif. Stile: fotografico pubblicitario pulito, mai screenshot finti dell'interfaccia prodotto (quelli sono sempre screenshot reali, come da convenzione già in uso).

## Asset 1 — Hero Facebook/Instagram feed (1:1, 1080×1080)
**Uso:** post ads Meta, copertina Reel R4.
**Prompt:** "Advertising photograph, clean and photorealistic commercial style, warm cream and burnt-orange color palette. A smiling woman in her 40s, pet boarding facility owner, standing in a bright sunny dog kennel facility, one happy golden retriever sitting calmly beside her. Soft natural daylight, shallow depth of field, blurred green outdoor kennels in background. No text, no logos, no screens visible."

## Asset 2 — Copertina TikTok/Reels/Stories (9:16, 1080×1920)
**Uso:** copertina video T1-T4 (Pillar dolore), thumbnail TikTok.
**Prompt:** "Advertising photograph, clean photorealistic commercial style, warm tones. Close-up of two hands holding an open paper notebook full of crossed-out handwritten dates and messy corrections, a smartphone with visible notification badges resting beside it on a wooden desk. Soft window light, shallow depth of field, slightly anxious cluttered mood but still clean composition. No readable app UI, no logos."

## Asset 3 — Banner Google Display/Performance Max (16:9, 1920×1080)
**Uso:** asset group Performance Max "prima/dopo", banner display remarketing.

Left half: chaotic desk with scattered paper receipts, a calculator, crumpled sticky notes, warm messy light. Right half: same desk but tidy and organized, a closed laptop, a neat cup of coffee, a small potted plant, calm warm light. Clear vertical dividing line between the two halves. No text, no logos, no screens."

## Asset 4 — Immagine storia del brand (16:9, per Performance Max e post D1-style)

Wide exterior shot of a bright, well-kept countryside dog boarding facility in Italy, green fenced outdoor play area, three happy dogs of different breeds playing on grass, small wooden kennel houses in soft focus background. Warm cream and orange color grading. No people close-up, no text, no logos."

## Video — hook TikTok T1 (b-roll, nessun attore/testimonial necessario)
**Uso:** apertura video T1 (`piano-tiktok.md`), 9:16, 6-10s.
**Prompt:** "Close-up shot of a paper notebook page, two overlapping handwritten booking dates circled in red pen, a hand nervously flipping the page. Cut to a bright, tidy pet boarding facility interior, calm and orderly. Warm natural light, photorealistic, no on-screen text, no visible app UI."

**Come generarli:** stesso flusso già in uso nel progetto (Pollinations.ai o strumento equivalente), poi ritaglio/ottimizzazione formato per piattaforma come già fatto per `marketing/social/assets/post-*.png`. Una volta generati, salvarli in `marketing/social/assets/` con naming coerente (es. `campagna-hero-01.png`) e referenziarli nei rispettivi piani.
